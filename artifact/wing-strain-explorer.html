What this configuration is telling you

Thin-walled composite section with classical laminate theory, Euler–Bernoulli beam with St Venant torsion, two symmetric mounts solved by statics. Strain at the gauge includes bending about both axes, Poisson contraction, torsional and transverse shear flow, and grid-length averaging. Figures match the accompanying Python package to three significant figures.
